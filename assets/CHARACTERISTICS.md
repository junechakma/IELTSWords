## Known near-duplicates
- `happy-mustard.png` and `content-mustard.png` are visually almost identical (same yellow blob smiling with eyes closed).
- `joyful-coral.png` and `ecstatic-coral.png` are the exact same source image (dancing orange blob).

## Suggested renames for weak fits (optional)
- `sad-blue.png` → grumpy/upset feels more accurate (arms out, frowning, not visibly "sad")
- `shocked-blue.png` → mild expression, could be renamed "curious" or "casual"
- `scared-orchid.png` → reads as "grumpy/annoyed" (crossed arms, frown), not fear
- `proud-giraffe.png` → giraffe looks "content/happy", not specifically proud
- `surprised-indigo.png` → mild expression, could be "curious" or "pointing"
