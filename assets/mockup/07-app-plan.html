The app trains a habit, not memory: swap the plain word you would normally write for the Band 8 one, in the right paragraph (Intro, Overview, Body) of each Task 1 chart type. Onboarding follows mockup 5 (Poppins, full-colour slides); the app follows mockup 3 (cream, Outfit), with the tile board from mockup 4, stickers from mockup 6 and wavy bands from mockup 2. No scores, no streaks — activity lives in the practice heatmap.
